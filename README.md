# Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) Optimization

Welcome to this repository! This project focuses on optimizing Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) to solve complex physical problems more efficiently and accurately. 

## The Challenge
Traditional machine learning models often struggle when dealing with complex physical systems unless they have massive amounts of training data. PINNs solve this by embedding known physical laws directly into the network. However, training them can be computationally heavy and notoriously difficult to balance.

## Our Approach
This project tackles the training bottlenecks in standard PINNs. We focus on advanced optimization strategies to make the network learn faster, stabilize training, and generalize better to unseen scenarios. Key highlights include:
- **Adaptive Activation Functions:** Allowing the network to dynamically adjust during training to capture intricate physical behaviors.
- **Causal Training Techniques:** Ensuring the model respects the natural flow of causality, which significantly stabilizes the learning process (featuring custom architectures like MABUR-PINN).

## Real-World Impact
By optimizing how these networks train, we can simulate complex engineering and physical systems with significantly less data and lower computational costs. This bridges the gap between purely data-driven models and traditional scientific computing, offering a fast, reliable tool for predictive modeling in industries ranging from renewable energy to aerospace.
